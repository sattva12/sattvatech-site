Manufacturing

Lean production, from a plan, not a whiteboard.

Most shops run the floor on spreadsheets, a whiteboard and somebody's memory. Lean puts the run on one plan: every step in order, the people it needs, the rate it should hit and the parts it uses. When the floor falls behind, you see it the same day, not at the end of the month. The same Lean runs a farm's season.

Less waiting.

Each step starts when the one before it is done and the people are free. Samana flags the day two steps want the same person.

Less waste.

Order what the plan uses, when it uses it. No rush freight, no shelf of parts nobody needed.

Nothing done twice.

Every change is a revision. The floor works from the issued plan and taps Got it when they have seen it.

Lean · the plan

Steps, people and dates, worked out for you.

Put in the steps of a run and how many people each takes. Lean lays them on the calendar Monday to Friday, keeps them inside the window the run has to finish in, and moves everything after a step when that step moves.

  • Drag a start and the steps after it follow
  • People each day, with the busiest day and who is double-booked
  • Go puts a step on the crew's list; Verify closes it once it is checked
Lean · rates and hours

Planned rate against real rate, on every step.

Each step carries the rate it should run at: units an hour, pieces a person-hour. Log what the floor really did and Samana draws it under the plan, green at or above, red below. Hours by week sit against the limit, so overtime shows up before it is paid.

  • Rates in the step's own units
  • Actuals against plan, as soon as days are logged
  • Hours by week with the line you set
Lean farming

The same plan runs a growing season.

Field prep, planting, harvest and drying are steps like any other. Lean hands them to the crew's phones in Farmmetric, our compliance app for licensed growers, and the crew checks them off in the field.

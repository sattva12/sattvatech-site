Flow and Lean have no limit on jobs or runs. A finished job stops counting as active. You pay for people: each person counts once in each business unit they work in, and every person sees only the parts they have access to. On Flow and Lean a seat can go anywhere, in Samana itself or on a crew portal; on Free and Takeoff, extra seats are for crew on the Takeoff portal. Plans are bought inside the app: start free, then pick a plan in Settings. You pay by card through Stripe and can cancel any time.
